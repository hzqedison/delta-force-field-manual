GUIDE 01 / EXTRACTION

Survive the first
decision, not just the first fight.

An extraction run is a chain of decisions. Keep the chain simple: know why you are entering, what would make you leave, and where the exit idea lives in your head.

FIELD NOTERisk grows quietly.

Every extra room, fight, and item adds another chance to lose the run. Treat information as loot too.

01 / BEFORE DEPLOYMENT

Set a success condition.

Choose one reason for the run: learn a route, bring out a specific kind of resource, or practice a fight. A narrow goal makes the leave-or-stay decision visible.

01Route

Pick a simple first path and a fallback direction.

02Threshold

Decide what result is already “enough” before the match gets loud.

03Exit

Keep an extraction option in mind; do not wait until the final minute to think about leaving.

02 / WHILE YOU ARE IN

Information beats one more fight.

Listen for what the map is telling you. If the route is no longer quiet, reduce exposure. If your inventory is full of useful items, the value of the next room may be lower than the risk of opening it.

“The cleanest run is not the one with the most noise. It is the one where every risk had a reason.”
03 / EVIDENCE BOUNDARY

What this page can—and cannot—claim.

Delta Force is described by the official Steam listing as a tactical operator-based extraction experience with dynamic maps, loot, bosses, and multiple extraction routes. The checklist above is practical editorial guidance, not an official ruleset. Patch-specific mechanics should be checked against current official notes.
