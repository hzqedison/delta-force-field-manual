GUIDE 02 / ORIENTATION

Choose a role
the team can read.

The official store listing frames Delta Force around operators, tactical tools, and large-scale combined arms warfare. Start by making your intent obvious to the squad.

PUSH

Take space

Move the team’s line forward when the next objective is clear. Call the direction, not just the target.

HOLD

Keep the lane

Protect the route the team already owns. Good holding is visible through calm information and fewer surprises.

SUPPORT

Make the play possible

Stay close enough to reinforce the next decision. The best support action is often the one that arrives before the request.

USEFUL PROMPT“What is our next objective, and what does the team need from me?”

Mode names, maps, operators, and balance details can change with updates. This page keeps the advice at the level of readable team intent and points to the official listing for the current high-level description.
